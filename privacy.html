Yleisperiaate

Arjen apurilla ei ole omaa palvelinta eikä käyttäjätiliä. Tallentamasi tiedot (perheenjäsenet, menot, muistikirja, kauppalista, kotityöt, ateriat ja reseptit, laskurit, kyytipyynnöt, tähdet ja viikkorahat) tallennetaan puhelimeesi. Jos otat käyttöön perheen jakamisen, tiedot synkronoidaan Applen iCloud-palvelun (CloudKit) kautta perheen perustajan iCloud-tilille ja niille perheenjäsenille, jotka perustaja kutsuu. Samalla tavalla synkronoidaan perheenjäsenten, menojen ja reseptien kuvat sekä kunkin henkilön omat asetukset (ulkoasu, kieli, ilmoitusajat, näkymien valinnat, kumppani ja kotipaikka), jotka siirtyvät saman henkilön muille laitteille. Someset Oy ei näe eikä käsittele näitä tietoja.

Kolmannet osapuolet

Sovellus käyttää seuraavia ulkopuolisia palveluja, jotka käsittelevät vain niille tarpeellista tietoa:

  • Apple (iCloud / CloudKit, App Store): perheen jaetun datan synkronointi perheen laitteille ja Apple Watchiin, tilausten ja ostojen käsittely sekä kerran päivässä tehtävä tarkistus, onko App Storessa uudempi versio sovelluksesta (vain sovelluksen tunniste lähetetään). Katso Applen tietosuojakäytäntö.
  • RevenueCat: tilauksen tila ja voimassaolo nimettömällä perhetunnisteella sekä ostotapahtumien tiedot. Katso RevenueCatin tietosuojakäytäntö.
  • Open-Meteo: sääennuste pyöristettyjen koordinaattien tai kirjoitetun paikkakunnan perusteella. Katso Open-Meteon käyttöehdot.

Sovellus ei myy eikä jaa tietoja mainostarkoituksiin, eikä se käytä mainontaa, seurantaa tai analytiikkaa.

Tietojen säilytys ja poistaminen

Voit poistaa puhelimen tiedot sovelluksen asetuksista (Tyhjennä kaikki tiedot) tai poistamalla sovelluksen. Widgetien tiedot tyhjenevät samalla, ja kellon tiedot poistuvat, kun poistat sovelluksen kellosta. Kokeilujakson alkamisaika säilyy avainnipussa sovelluksen poiston jälkeenkin, mutta se ei sisällä henkilötietoja.

Perheen jaetut tiedot ovat perustajan iCloudissa. Perustajan tekemä tyhjennys poistaa ne koko perheeltä. Kutsuttu jäsen voi poistua perheestä tyhjentämällä tietonsa, jolloin perheen tiedot säilyvät muilla. Perustajan poistama jäsen irrottautuu perheestä, kun hänen sovelluksensa seuraavan kerran synkronoi. Hänen laitteelleen jo tulleet tiedot jäävät sinne, mutta ne eivät enää päivity. iCloud-tiedot voi poistaa myös iPhonen asetuksista (Asetukset → oma nimi → iCloud → Hallitse tallennustilaa).

Oikeutesi

Someset Oy ei ylläpidä omaa tietokantaa käyttäjistä, joten suurin osa EU:n tietosuoja-asetuksen (GDPR) mukaisista oikeuksistasi (tarkastus, oikaisu ja poisto) toteutuu suoraan puhelimesi ja oman iCloud-tilisi kautta. Apuri — Privacy Policy

Updated 3 November 2026

The app is called Apuri, and Arjen apuri in Finnish. This policy covers it under both names.

Principle

Apuri has no server of its own and no user account. The information you enter (family members, plans, notebook, shopping list, chores, meals and recipes, counters, ride requests, stars and allowances) is stored on your phone. If you turn on family sharing, it is synced through Apple iCloud (CloudKit) to the family founder’s iCloud account and to the family members the founder invites. Each person’s own settings (appearance, language, notification times, view choices, partner and home location) and the photos added to family members, plans and recipes are synced the same way, so they also reach that person’s other devices. Someset Oy cannot see or process this information.

What is processed and why

  • Calendar events: read from the phone’s calendar to show plans and things to pack. With family sharing on, upcoming events from Family calendars and calendars assigned to a family member are shared with the family through iCloud.
  • Reminders: shopping list sync, in Apple’s Reminders app and your own iCloud.
  • Contacts: choosing a partner and importing birthdays, only when you ask for them. When you choose a partner, the app gets only the person you pick. Importing birthdays reads, with your permission, the names and birthdays in the phone’s contacts, and once you confirm the import, the birthdays are stored in the app. No other contact details are read. The partner’s name and number and imported birthdays are synced like the rest of the family’s data if family sharing is on.
  • Location: fetched at low accuracy and rounded to about 1 km before being sent to the Open-Meteo weather service. You can also type a place name. Your home location is shared with the family if family sharing is on.
  • Photos and camera (optional): family member photos, special-day attachments, recipe photos, home display photos and reading a recipe from a photo. The app gets only the photos you pick, and they are copied into the app. With family sharing on, family member, special-day and recipe photos are synced like the rest of the family’s data through the founder’s iCloud (CloudKit) to the family’s devices. They are also included in a backup file you export yourself. Home display photos stay on the device. Recipe text is read on the device, and the photo is neither saved nor sent.
  • Notifications: scheduled on the phone.
  • Notebook: home details, codes and passwords, phone numbers and meter readings that you type in. Stored on the phone. With family sharing on, the notebook is synced to the family’s devices, including entries marked secret. They are shown hidden and adults can reveal them, but they are not separately encrypted. The View only role does not see them in the app or in its own backup, but they reach its device with the rest of the data. A child’s device and Apple Watch do not show the notebook, and the home display does not show secret entries. In a backup file you export yourself the notebook is in plain text, and the app warns you before exporting if it has secret entries.
  • Apple Watch (optional): the day’s plans, chores, stars, shopping list and ride requests on the watch and watch face, and confirmations from the watch. The watch fetches family data from the founder’s iCloud (CloudKit) and keeps only what it needs on the watch. The notebook does not reach the watch. Confirmations travel through iCloud to the family’s devices. When a watch joins the family with a code, the invitation link and family member identifiers are stored in iCloud’s public database behind a six-digit code. The code is valid for 10 minutes and is removed when the code window is closed. If the window is left open, the app removes the code at the next sync after it expires. The code contains no family data.
  • Widgets (optional): the day’s plans, chores, stars, shopping list and rides on the iPhone Home Screen and Lock Screen. Widget data is stored on the phone, in storage shared by the app and its widgets. A widget on the Lock Screen also shows data while the phone is locked. Confirmations from widget buttons are sent to iCloud if family sharing is on.
  • Subscription: Apple (App Store) and RevenueCat. The subscription is linked to the family with a random, anonymous family identifier that contains no names or other family data.
  • Rating prompt: the app may ask for a rating in Apple’s own dialog. Only the number of days of use and the time of the last request are kept on the device. They don’t leave the device, and the app doesn’t see your rating.
  • Trial information: the start date of the 14-day trial is kept in the phone’s Keychain so that the trial does not restart. It survives reinstalling the app and contains no personal data.

Third parties

  • Apple (iCloud / CloudKit, App Store): family sharing sync to the family’s devices and Apple Watch, purchases, and a once-a-day check in the App Store for a newer version of the app (only the app’s identifier is sent). See Apple’s privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat: subscription status with an anonymous family identifier and purchase records. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.
  • Open-Meteo: weather forecasts from rounded coordinates or a typed place name. See Open-Meteo’s terms.

The app does not sell or share data for advertising and uses no advertising, tracking or analytics.

Retention and deletion

You can delete the data on your phone in the app’s settings (Clear all data) or by deleting the app. Widget data is cleared at the same time, and watch data is removed when you delete the app from the watch. The trial start date stays in the Keychain but contains no personal data. Shared family data is stored in the founder’s iCloud: clearing data on the founder’s phone removes it for the whole family, while an invited member who clears their data leaves the family and the data stays with the others. A member removed by the founder is detached from the family the next time their app syncs. Data already on their device stays there but no longer updates. iCloud data can also be removed in iPhone Settings (your name → iCloud → Manage Storage).

Your rights

Someset Oy keeps no database of users, so most of your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (access, rectification and erasure) are exercised directly on your phone and in your own iCloud account. For questions, contact info@rakennuskuntoon.fi.
